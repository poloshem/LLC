Who we are

About Lord's Last Call Church

A Bible-centered community called to worship God, grow in faith, serve people, and share the hope of Jesus Christ.

Discover Our Story
Our story

Rooted in faith, gathered for purpose.

Lord's Last Call Church (Luong Mogik Mar Nyasaye) is a community of believers committed to the teachings of Jesus Christ and the authority of the Bible. We exist to worship God, grow in faith, and make a positive impact in our families, communities, and the world.

Our fellowship is built on prayer, discipleship, compassionate service, and a shared commitment to taking the Gospel to all nations. We welcome people at every stage of their faith journey.

View Church Constitution
Mission, vision, and values

Our calling is simple: follow Jesus, make disciples, and serve with love.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and the Word of God, make disciples, nurture believers through fellowship, Bible study, prayer and the Holy Spirit, serve humanity with compassion, and extend Christ's work through evangelism, missions and church planting.

Our Vision

To see a worldwide, united and spiritually mature body of believers transformed into the image of Jesus Christ, living according to God's Word and prepared for His glorious return.

Our Values

We value biblical truth, Spirit-led worship, authentic fellowship, compassionate service, faithful stewardship, family discipleship, and the mission of God in every nation.

Leadership & Structure

Servant leadership helps our church remain accountable, organized, and focused on Christ.

P

Senior Pastor

Provides spiritual vision, preaching, pastoral care, and overall shepherding of the congregation.

E

Church Elders

Support doctrine, governance, prayer, discipleship, and the spiritual health of the church.

M

Ministry Leaders

Coordinate worship, Bible study, youth, women, outreach, and mission activities.

C

Members & Volunteers

Serve together with their gifts to build up the church and bless the wider community.
