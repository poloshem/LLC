Full Statement of Faith

Our beliefs are centered on Scripture and the good news of salvation through Jesus Christ.

The Holy Bible

We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word, trustworthy in all it teaches and authoritative for faith and life.

God the Father

We believe in one living and true God, Creator and Sustainer of all things, perfect in love, holiness, justice, and mercy.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully human, who died for our sins, rose again, and reigns forever.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts, regenerates, indwells, guides, empowers, and gifts believers for godly living and service.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, received through repentance, new birth, and a life transformed by God.

The Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, called to worship, discipleship, fellowship, prayer, evangelism, and compassionate service.

Baptism & Communion

We practice baptism by immersion as a public expression of faith and remember Christ's sacrifice through the Lord's Supper.

Christ's Return

We believe in the personal, visible return of Jesus Christ, the resurrection, final judgment, and eternal life with God.

The Ten Commandments

God's commandments teach us to love Him wholeheartedly and love our neighbors as ourselves.

You shall have no other gods before the Lord.

You shall not make or worship idols.

You shall not misuse the name of the Lord.

Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy.

Honor your father and your mother.

You shall not murder.

You shall not commit adultery.

You shall not steal.

You shall not give false testimony.

You shall not covet what belongs to your neighbor.
