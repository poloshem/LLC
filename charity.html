    Faith made visible through love

    Compassion that helps hope grow

    We believe every neighbor deserves to be seen, welcomed, and supported with dignity. Together, we seek to make Christ's love tangible in our communities.

    Explore our charity work
    Our calling

    Love is more than words. It shows up.

    Scripture calls us to “carry each other’s burdens” (Galatians 6:2) and reminds us that faith is expressed in care for those facing hardship (James 1:27). This calling moves us to listen, stand alongside one another, and offer practical help where it is needed.

    Our charity work is rooted in the belief that no one should have to face a difficult season alone. We seek to serve without judgment, honor each person's dignity, and build a church family where compassion becomes a shared practice.

    Care that opens doors and strengthens lives

    We focus on practical support and faithful companionship, trusting that small acts of love can help carry hope into the future.

    Education

    Bursaries that nurture possibility

    When financial hardship threatens a learner’s education, a bursary can help keep the door to learning open. Through bursary support, we seek to ease that burden and encourage students to keep growing, learning, and pursuing the gifts God has placed within them.

    Every opportunity to learn can strengthen a family and bless a community for generations.

    Care & belonging

    Standing with widows and orphans

    Widows and children who have lost a parent can carry grief and practical worries that are often unseen. We seek to meet them with tenderness, respect, prayer, and meaningful support—reminding each person that they are not forgotten and do not walk alone.

    “A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows” is how Psalm 68:5 describes God's heart.

    Inclusion

    Making room for people with disabilities

    Every person is made in the image of God and belongs in the life of the community. We seek to welcome people with disabilities, listen to what they need, and offer practical care and fellowship without reducing anyone to a diagnosis or limitation.

    True community makes space for every person to participate, contribute, and be valued.

    A shared ministry

    We serve one another as one family.

    Charity is not only the work of a few; it is a calling we share. A visit, a listening ear, a prayer, a contribution toward school needs, or help with an everyday task can all become signs of care.

    Jesus said, “Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me” (Matthew 25:40). We hold this teaching close as we serve—with humility, compassion, and gratitude.

    “Let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth.”

    1 John 3:18
    Testimonies

    Stories of hope, shared with care.

    Behind every act of support is a person, a family, and a story worth honoring. We look forward to sharing testimonies from members and families who choose to tell how encouragement, education support, or practical care has made a difference in their journey.

    To protect privacy and dignity, each testimony will be shared only with the person's permission. No personal story is published here yet.

    There is room to care for one another.

    Join us in prayer, encouragement, and practical service. Every act of compassion can help someone feel seen and supported.
